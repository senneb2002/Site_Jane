About Jane

A Safe Space for Growth & Healing

I have an extensive background working with adults and couples. I believe most conflicts within a relationship come down to effective communication skills, and an openness to look at one's own needs for self-growth.

"Our early childhood experiences most often shape our belief systems, and these beliefs may not be moving us in the direction of our dreams and goals."

Fear of failure, fear of judgement, fear of change — these are often the cause for dreams unrealized. Those I work with should have a strong desire for change, and be willing to sit with their emotions, practice skills learned in session, and commit to living their best lives.
